# Changelog

This repo started as an entry for the AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon on LabLab
(September 2026). The Colosseum work begins on 2026-10-10. The two are kept apart below
so it is clear what existed before and what is new.

Commit links point to `github.com/elghaly-dev/elghaly-35-voice`. Dates are UTC.

## Colosseum (from 2026-10-10)

Pitch: **Iris helps people understand suspicious wallet messages and decide what to do
next, without connecting a wallet.**

### 2026-10-10: Check this message or link ([#7](https://github.com/elghaly-dev/elghaly-35-voice/pull/7))

- New first panel on the page. You paste a message or link and get a status, the
  reason, the evidence (the words or host that matched) and the next step.
- Statuses are `known-malicious`, `suspicious` or `unknown`. There is no "safe" status.
- The check runs in the browser and makes no network call. Links are shown as defanged
  text and never opened, and there is no wallet connect.
- Pasted recovery words (12 or 24 words from the BIP-39 list) get **Stop**, and the box
  is cleared.
- Fixture sheet: 25 synthetic messages (10 scam, 10 normal or advice, 5 uncertain). The
  results are in `docs/FIXTURES.md`, which is regenerated by `npm run fixtures` and
  checked by `npm test`. Scam: 10 of 10 detected, 0 missed. Normal and advice: 1 false
  positive out of 10.

### 2026-10-10: Reliable judge path ([#6](https://github.com/elghaly-dev/elghaly-35-voice/pull/6))

- The link cards are rendered into the page by the server, so they show before any
  script or voice call runs.
- The header shows two separate checks, **Server** (`/api/health`) and **Voice**.
  `/api/health` does not call AssemblyAI.
- Before the mic turns on, the page shows: "Your speech is sent to AssemblyAI for
  transcription. Never speak a seed phrase or private key." You can then turn on the
  mic or type instead.
- While voice connects, a step list shows each stage: server, desk policy, voice token,
  microphone, audio engine and voice session.
- **Text mode** opens with the reason when any of those stages fails. It answers from
  fixed rules in the page and sends nothing.
- The server starts without `ASSEMBLYAI_API_KEY`. In that case the voice token route
  answers 503 and the page stays in text mode. Before this change, a missing key
  stopped the server.
- A link to the recorded demo sits at the top of the page.
- Added server tests (`node --test`) and a CI workflow.
